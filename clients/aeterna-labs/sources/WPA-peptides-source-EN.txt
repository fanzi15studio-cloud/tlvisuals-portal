Each peptide profile is broken down into five distinct sections:

        clinical or preclinical literature.

     ●​ The Real-World Context ("Anecdotal") Observations from independent

     ●​ The Research Protocols (Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced) Tiered dosing
        schedules designed to match research goals.

            ○​ Includes: Specific dosage (mg and mcg), frequency, and cycle duration.

            ○​ Note: Protocols move from conservative (safety-first) to aggressive
               (efficacy-first).

     ●​ The Support Stack ("Supplements & diet") Nutritional and supplemental adjuncts
        that may synergize with the peptide or mitigate specific side effects.

     ●​ The Safety Signal ("Cautions") A summary of known side effects,
        contraindications, or adverse events.

            ○​ Source: Derived from both clinical literature and common anecdotal reports.

            ○​ Purpose: To identify "red flags" and expected physiological responses
               during research.

5-Amino-1MQ
What it is / research focus: 5-Amino-1MQ is technically a "small molecule," not a
peptide, which means it can be taken as a capsule. It is a specific inhibitor of an enzyme
called NNMT.
     ●​ The Problem: As we age, our fat cells start overproducing NNMT. This enzyme
        impairs fat burning and slows down muscle repair.
     ●​ The Goal: To block this enzyme, forcing the fat cells to shrink and the muscle cells
        to repair themselves.
How it works (The Parking Brake Story): Think of your metabolism as a car.
     ●​ The Problem (NNMT): As you get older, your body starts pulling the Parking Brake
        (NNMT) while you are trying to drive. You can press the gas (exercise) and cut the
        fuel (diet), but because the brake is on, the car won't move, and you just waste
        energy.
     ●​ The Solution (5-Amino-1MQ): This compound reaches down and releases the
        parking brake.
     ●​ The Result: Suddenly, the same amount of gas (exercise) makes the car go much
        faster. The engine stops struggling, and the fuel efficiency (fat burning) skyrockets.
The "Strange" Fact (The Muscle Miracle): Most "fat burners" eat away at muscle, but
5-Amino-1MQ does the opposite.
     ●​ The Study: In famous experiments, aged mice given this compound became
        stronger and had better muscle endurance even without exercising. It seems to
        wake up "Satellite Cells" (muscle stem cells) that usually go dormant in old age,
        allowing old muscles to heal like young muscles.
In simple terms: It is a "Metabolic Brake Release." It stops your fat cells from fighting
against your weight loss and helps muscles stay young.
Anecdotal:
     ●​ "The Oral Advantage": Because this survives digestion, researchers love that
        they don't have to inject it. It is almost exclusively found as capsules.

What it is / research focus: Adipotide is a very different kind of research compound. Most diet
aids work by making you feel full or speeding up your metabolism. Adipotide works by "starving"
the fat cells directly.
How it works (The Garden Hose Story): Imagine your body fat is like a big garden. For the
grass (fat) to grow, it needs water from a garden hose (blood vessels).
     ●​ The Target: Adipotide is like a smart clamp. It is designed to find only the garden hoses
         that feed your body fat.
     ●​ The Action: It clamps those hoses shut so no blood can get through.
     ●​ The Result: Without fresh blood, the fat cells cannot survive. They shrink and die away.
Why is this serious? Because the fat cells are actually dying inside the body, the body has to
clean up the mess. This is a lot of work for the system, which is why researchers often report
feeling "flu-like" symptoms or getting dehydrated.
In simple terms: It cuts off the blood supply to fat cells. The fat cells run out of food and die.
Anecdotal: This is considered a "heavy duty" researcher tool. It is known for working fast, but it
is not comfortable. Many reports mention feeling tired or having a tummy ache while the fat is
breaking down. Drinking a lot of water is non-negotiable to help the kidneys flush out the
waste.

How it works (The Battery Meter Story): Inside your cells, there is a sensor called AMPK.
Think of AMPK as your body's "Battery Meter."
     ●​ Normal Exercise: When you run or lift weights, your battery meter drops. Your body

     ●​ The Trick: AICAR enters the cell and pulls a prank. It manually turns the "Low Battery"
        light on.
     ●​ The Result: Your body panics (in a good way) and starts burning fuel to recharge the

        plants" (mitochondria) in the muscles to prepare for this fake workout.
Why is this researched? Scientists are studying it to see if it can help people who cannot
exercise (like the elderly or injured) stay fit. Athletes have studied it for endurance because it
might help muscles run longer without getting tired.
In simple terms: It flips the "exercise switch" in your cells. It makes the body burn fat and build
endurance without actually moving.
Anecdotal: This is not a "feel good" compound. Researchers often report that it feels like doing
a hard workout. "Hot flashes," sweating, and feeling winded or tired (even while sitting still) are
common reports because the metabolism is working so hard.
